Research preview. Answers are probabilities over the options you give, from a small model running locally. Measured through this server on held-out benchmarks, it answers “Is there a <common object>?” correctly 79–88% of the time (POPE; COCO's 80 object names such as person, cup, couch) and ranks photo quality well (KonIQ-10k), but everyday yes/no questions such as “Is there a man?” or “Is the woman smiling?” are at chance (50% on VQAv2), and multiple-choice knowledge questions are weak (43% on A-OKVQA). A choice question always picks one of your options, even if none fits.
